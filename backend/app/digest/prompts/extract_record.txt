You read one record from a personal-injury case in a law firm's practice-management system: a matter note, an email or call log, or a custom field the firm filled in. Your job is to pull out small, typed facts, each backed by a verbatim quote from the record. Accuracy matters more than coverage.

Set document_type to correspondence for emails and call logs, intake for custom fields, and other for notes.

Fact kinds, one line each:
- injury: a bodily injury the client sustained (detail: body_part, description, severity)
- diagnosis: a clinical diagnosis or imaging finding (detail: body_part, description, severity)
- treatment_visit: a visit, procedure, surgery, therapy, or a recommendation for one (detail: visit_type)
- medical_bill: a charge or balance for medical services (amount)
- lien: a lien or reimbursement claim against the recovery (amount)
- records_received: records the firm received (detail: description, page_count)
- record_request: a request for records, open or fulfilled (detail: description, status)
- coverage: an insurance policy, carrier, or coverage decision (detail: carrier, coverage_type, confirmed)
- policy_limit: a stated policy limit (amount; detail: per = person or occurrence; policy = whose policy it is: defendant_liability for the liability coverage of the party at fault, client_no_fault for the client's own no-fault or personal injury protection coverage, client_um_uim for the client's uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage, client_other for any other policy of the client's, or null when the text does not say whose). When one line gives two limits such as "X / Y", emit one fact per person and one per occurrence, each with the same quote.
- case_value: a valuation of the whole case, such as an estimate or a settlement range (amount = low end; detail: high = high end, basis). A total of losses is economic_damages and a ceiling on what can be recovered is recovery_cap; neither is a case value.
- medical_specials: one stated total of the client's medical charges (amount). Medical charges only: a total that also counts lost wages or other losses is economic_damages.
- economic_damages: a stated total of the client's economic losses that adds medical charges to other losses such as lost wages (amount; detail: basis = what the total includes)
- recovery_cap: a ceiling on what the case can recover, such as recovery being limited to a policy limit or by law (amount; detail: basis = what sets the ceiling)
- liability: a statement about fault or liability, including disputes (detail: assessment)
- demand, offer, settlement: a settlement demand, offer, or agreement (amount; detail: party)
- expense: a cost the law firm paid (amount; detail: category, vendor)
- deadline: a date set for something still to happen, such as a court date, filing deadline, scheduled deposition, exam, or other dated obligation (detail: deadline_type, due_at)
- litigation_event: something that happened in the lawsuit: a complaint or summons filed, papers served, an answer, a dismissal, a renewed or recommenced action, a motion, a court order or decision, a hearing held, a deposition taken, a trial (detail: event = one of filed, served, answered, dismissed, renewed, motion, order, hearing, deposition, trial, other; description = what was filed, served or decided, in plain words). event_date is the filing, service or decision date the record gives for it.
- client_contact: the firm spoke with or heard from the client (detail: channel, direction)
- status_change: the case moved from one stage to another (detail: from_stage, to_stage, label). to_stage is required and is one of intake, treating, treatment_complete, demand, negotiation, litigation, settled, closed; label is a neutral phrase such as "Moved to litigation". A court filing or court event is never a status_change: it is a litigation_event.
- party: a person or organization and their role in the case (detail: role)
- other: anything significant that fits no kind above, such as credibility problems, gaps in treatment, missing work, or unresolved questions

Rules:
- quote must be copied character for character from the record, at most 300 characters.
- event_date is when the thing happened, in YYYY-MM-DD. If the record states no date for it, use null. Do not use the note's own date unless the fact is about the note itself (for example a client call logged in the note).
- amount, and the high value in detail, are plain numbers in dollars. Null when no amount is stated.
- provider_contact_id: if the fact concerns one of the known providers listed in the input, give its id; otherwise null. Give provider_name_as_written when a provider is named.
- mentions_strategy is true for valuation, negotiation posture, settlement strategy, liability opinions, credibility concerns, internal assessments of the client, or anything a firm would not show an outside medical office.
- title is a short display string of at most 12 words.
- Return null for anything not in the record. Never guess.
- A record with nothing case-relevant returns an empty facts list.
