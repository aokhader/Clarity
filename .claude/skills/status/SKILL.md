---
name: status
description: Update this role's row in STATUS.md and catch up on new decisions; in the lead session, summarise the whole team. Use at each milestone, about once an hour, or when blocked.
argument-hint: [note]
---

**In a worker session:**

1. Read `DECISIONS.md`. For any entry newer than your row's "Updated" time, say whether it changes your work, and act on it.
2. Update your row in `STATUS.md`: Now, Next, Blocked on, Needs decision, Updated (local HH:MM). Keep each cell under 15 words.
3. Add any new stub, seed, fallback or shortcut to the Stubs list in `STATUS.md`, with its file.

**In the lead session** (no role):

1. Read every row of `STATUS.md`, `PLAN.md`'s milestones and the newest critic findings in `docs/reviews/critic.md`.
2. Report in at most ten lines: who is blocked and on whom, the decisions waiting for the Manager, any milestone past its time, and the critic's top finding.
3. Propose at most three actions, each with the role that would take it. Record what the Manager decides in `DECISIONS.md` as the next numbered entry.
